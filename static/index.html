Own Song Engine

Compose original music and experiment with synthesized Telugu or English vocals — no paid generation API.

Reference audio or video

Upload a short video or audio file to extract its soundtrack for listening and reference. MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, WEBM or FLAC; maximum 30 MB.

Note: this extracts and previews audio only. It does not yet recreate the same melody with Telugu vocals.

Natural AI singing requires an ACE-Step inference server configured by the app administrator. The local option uses the current experimental synthesizer.

About the vocal engine

Vocals are generated by an original lightweight formant synthesizer, not a downloaded singing model or paid API. This is experimental singing synthesis, not yet natural human-quality singing; lyric pronunciation and Telugu syllable timing need further development. Child is a synthetic high-pitched tone profile, not a real child voice.
